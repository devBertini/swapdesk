# Swapdesk: flow map

One flow per row of `docs/SPEC.md` "Flows" (the criteria live there; IDs are the same). Statuses, dates and the scoreboard are written only by the maintainers' flow-map tool (not part of this repository), never by hand. A flow enters here before its code; its slice fills `Entrada` (the source files it goes through) and `Prova` (the tests that prove it, `<binary id>/<module path::test>`). Until then the engine reads an empty `Prova` as `sem prova`.
